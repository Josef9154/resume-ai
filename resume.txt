ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI):
Artificial intelligence is the broad field of building systems that perform tasks such as understanding language, recognizing patterns, and supporting decisions. AI includes machine learning, but not every AI system learns from data.

MACHINE LEARNING (ML):
Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence where models learn patterns from data to make predictions or classifications instead of relying only on explicitly written rules. A practical example is predicting customer churn from historical customer data.

DEEP LEARNING (DL):
Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn representations of data. It is commonly used for language, images, and speech. More complex is not automatically better; the problem, data, cost, and validation should drive the choice.
